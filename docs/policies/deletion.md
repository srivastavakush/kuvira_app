# Delete your MatchDrome account

In the app, open Profile → Help & Privacy → Delete account. Type DELETE and confirm. Account access is disabled immediately and a durable personal-data deletion request is created.

Without the app, email contact@kuvirasports.com with subject “Delete my MatchDrome account”, your registered phone number and a reply email. We will verify ownership without asking for an OTP or password.

Deletion removes personal profile, uploaded avatar/video, community posts and coaching data from active systems after processing. We aim to complete requests within 30 days. Pending bookings, payment records, support disputes and information legally required may be retained with restricted access. Backups expire under the hosting retention policy. Club owners should arrange operational handover; support can help protect ongoing bookings.

Deleting the account does not itself cancel a paid booking or request a refund. For those, contact support with the booking reference.
