# Cancellations & payment help

Send cancellation inquiries at least four hours before the event, match or court booking starts. Times shown without a timezone are interpreted in India Standard Time (UTC+05:30).

Open Help & Privacy, select Cancellation inquiry and choose your booking or registration. Include the issue and your reply email. Tickets are addressed to contact@kuvirasports.com. You can also email this address directly with the booking reference. Do not include card credentials, PINs or OTPs.

Submitting a ticket does not cancel a booking or guarantee a refund. Support reviews the applicable venue terms, timing and payment status, then communicates the outcome. Approved refunds are processed through the payment provider; actual bank-credit timing depends on the provider and bank. MatchDrome will not show a refund as completed without a verified reference.

Within four hours of the start, the in-app cancellation inquiry window is closed. You may still contact support about payment errors, safety issues, organiser cancellations or other assistance. Your applicable consumer rights are unaffected.

If money was debited but the app shows a pending payment, use Refresh payment status before retrying payment. Late payments for unavailable reservations are flagged for support review.
