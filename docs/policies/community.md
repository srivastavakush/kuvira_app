# Community rules

Be respectful and keep the community focused on sport. No harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, illegal content, scams, impersonation or sharing someone’s private information without permission.

Post only content you are entitled to share. Get permission before uploading identifiable footage of others. Do not target children or solicit private contact details.

Use Report on a post to flag a concern. Use Block player to hide their community posts; you can undo blocks from Help & Privacy. Blocking is a community visibility control, not an emergency service or a guarantee you will never encounter someone at a public venue.

Platform administrators review reports and can remove content. Serious or urgent concerns can also be emailed to contact@kuvirasports.com. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
