# Privacy policy

Effective 13 September 2026. Contact: contact@kuvirasports.com.

MatchDrome uses your phone number to authenticate your account; profile information and optional location to help you discover sports and players; and booking, order and payment references to provide services and support. Public profile details and community posts can be seen by other users. Do not post private contact or financial information.

Firebase/Google processes phone authentication and abuse-prevention data. PayU processes payment details. MatchDrome does not need your card PIN, bank password or OTP for support. Cloud hosting, database, email and AI providers process data needed to deliver their respective services. Club personnel receive information needed to operate their bookings, within their permissions.

When you select a photo or video, we upload that file for the requested feature. Match videos are private cloud objects used for analysis. Source videos are scheduled for deletion after successful analysis; reports and derived coaching information remain until you request deletion. Interrupted or failed uploads may remain until cleanup runs. Device-selected files are not deleted from your device. Only upload footage you have permission to share, including permission from identifiable participants.

AI analysis may be incomplete or inaccurate. Avoid uploading sensitive documents or information unrelated to sport. We use operational logs to investigate errors and abuse. Do not send credentials to support.

You can edit your profile, block community users and request account deletion in Help & Privacy. Deletion disables account access and queues removal of personal profile, community and coaching data. We aim to process requests within 30 days. Records needed for pending bookings, payments, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations may be retained with restricted access. Backup copies follow the hosting backup-retention cycle.

For access, correction, privacy concerns or deletion without the app, email contact@kuvirasports.com with the subject “MatchDrome privacy request”. We may verify account ownership; never send your password or OTP. Changes to this policy will be published on this page.
